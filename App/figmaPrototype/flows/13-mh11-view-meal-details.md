# 13 - MH-11 View meal details

## User story
As a user, I want to see meal times and menus so I can find options suitable for my party.

## Screen goal
Present meal schedule and menu details with filtering and clear readability.

## Key sections
1. Date and meal filters
2. Meal cards with time and menu summary
3. Dish details with dietary labels
4. CTA to continue to booking
